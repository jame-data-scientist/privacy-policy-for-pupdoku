Privacy Policy

Pupdoku

Android app Pupdoku (package com.pupdoku.game)
Effective date: 24 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 24 October 2026

The short version. Pupdoku has no accounts and no servers. Your puzzle progress stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what Google processes to show ads and to handle purchases.

  • No sign-up. You can play every level without creating an account.
  • Local progress. Levels, stars, coins, hints and settings are saved only on your device.
  • Ads. Google AdMob shows the banners and the optional reward video. To do that, Google receives your device's advertising ID.
  • Purchases. Coin packs are paid through Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • No trackers. No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising networks other than Google.

1. Who is responsible for your data

This policy covers the Pupdoku Android game (the “app”, “game”, “we”, “us”). The developer and data controller is the publisher of Pupdoku on Google Play, reachable at slothdevwork@gmail.com. If you have any question about this policy or about your data, contact us there first — most questions can be answered in a day or two.

2. What the app stores on your device

The app saves a single local file (pupdoku.cfg in the app's private storage). It contains:

  • your progress: current and highest unlocked level, score, best score, number of solved levels;
  • your in-game wallet: paw coins and hint count;
  • trophy statistics: three-star levels, hint-free solves, fast solves, clean streaks, achievements earned;
  • your settings: sound, vibration, pattern mode, whether the tutorial has been seen;
  • an ad bookkeeping value: how many reward videos you watched today, used only to enforce the daily limit.

This information never leaves your device and is not visible to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all of it permanently. It is not backed up by us, and it cannot be recovered once removed.

3. Information collected by third-party services

Pupdoku integrates three Google services. Each one processes data under Google's own privacy policy, and each one is described below.

3.1 Advertising — Google AdMob

The app shows banner ads and an optional reward video through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To request, deliver and measure ads, Google receives:

  • your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier provided by Google Play services) and the app-specific SDK instance ID;
  • technical information about the ad request: IP address, device type, operating system version, language, and app version;
  • ad interaction events, such as an impression, a click, or a completed reward video.

Google uses this to select ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure their performance, detect invalid traffic, and — only if you consent — personalise ads. If you decline personalised ads, you will still see ads, but they will be based on the context rather than on your advertising ID. We do not use advertising mediation or any other ad network, and we do not build advertising profiles of you ourselves.

3.2 Purchases — Google Play Billing

The paw coin packs are one-time in-app products sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment. To deliver your coins, the app receives a confirmation containing the product identifier and a purchase token. We never receive your name, email, address or card details, and we do not store purchase records on any server. Google retains transaction records according to its own policies and applicable tax law.

3.3 Achievements — Google Play Games (optional)

The trophy cabinet works fully offline. If you choose “Sign in with Google Play Games” in the Trophies screen, the app unlocks your earned achievements in Google Play Games so they appear in your Google profile. In that case Google processes your Play Games player identity and achievement state. Signing in is optional: every level, the store and all trophies remain available without it, and local trophies are kept either way. We do not receive or store your Google name, email or profile picture.

4. What we do not collect

  • No account, email address, phone number or password — there is no sign-up at all.
  • No precise or approximate location: the app never requests a location permission.
  • No contacts, photos, videos, files, calendar, microphone or camera access.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or behavioural profiling of our own.
  • No selling of personal data, and no sharing with data brokers.

The app declares only the internet, network-state and Play Billing permissions.

5. Why we are allowed to process this data

Where data protection law such as the GDPR applies, the legal bases are:

  • Consent — for personalised advertising. You are asked through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Legitimate interests — for non-personalised advertising needed to keep the game free, and for security and fraud prevention in ad delivery.
  • Contract — for processing a purchase you have made.

6. Your choices and rights

Reset or delete your advertising ID at any time: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it replaces your advertising ID with a string of zeros for every app on the device.

  • Ad personalisation: decline the consent prompt, or open Settings → Privacy options inside the game (shown where consent is required).
  • Local data: clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. This removes progress, coins and settings immediately and completely.
  • Achievements: sign out of Play Games, or remove the game's data from your Google account's Play Games settings.
  • Access, correction, deletion, objection or portability: because we hold no personal data on our side, there is usually nothing to access or delete beyond your device. Write to slothdevwork@gmail.com and we will help; if the data is held by Google, we will point you to the relevant Google control. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Data retention

We operate no server and keep no copies of player data. Local data remains on your device until you delete it. Data held by Google is retained according to Google's own retention periods, described in the Google Privacy Policy linked below.

8. Security

All communication between the app and Google's services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). The app's own data is stored in its private application directory, which other apps cannot read on a normal Android device. Remember that no method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

9. Children

Pupdoku is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app offers no accounts and no user-generated content, and its advertising is not configured for child-directed treatment. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us at slothdevwork@gmail.com and we will help you remove it.

10. International transfers

Google processes data in data centres around the world. Where data originating in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland is transferred outside those regions, Google relies on recognised safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Details are in Google's privacy documentation.
